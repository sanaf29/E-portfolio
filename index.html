About Me

A documentation-style page for my story, values, co-curricular interests, and professional growth.

『 my journey 』

My journey into computer science started with a curiosity about how systems work and how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. I grew up in Iran, where I learned the value of hard work, persistence, and continuous learning. Those early experiences shaped the way I approach challenges today, with patience and a willingness to keep improving.

I began my academic studies at the University of Tabriz, where I built my foundation in mathematics and programming. Later, I made the decision to transfer to York University’s Lassonde School of Engineering to continue my studies in an environment that encourages innovation, collaboration, and research.

This transition was an important step in my growth, both academically and personally. It challenged me to adapt to a new environment, think more independently, and push myself beyond my comfort zone.

Through my coursework and co-op experience, I have continued to develop my skills in programming, system design, and problem-solving. I have also gained a better understanding of how real-world systems are built, tested, and maintained, which has strengthened my interest in software engineering.

『 my vision as engineer 』

My vision as an engineer is to build reliable and intelligent systems that people can truly depend on in their everyday lives. Through my academic journey and co-op experience, I realized that engineering is not only about writing code, but about creating complete systems that are scalable, well-tested, and trustworthy.

What motivates me most is the idea that good engineering can prevent problems before they happen and create systems that work smoothly in the background. I want to contribute to solutions that make processes easier, reduce errors, and improve the overall experience for users and organizations.

My perspective has been shaped by working in both development and quality assurance environments. This experience helped me understand the full lifecycle of a system, from building features to ensuring their reliability through testing. I believe this combination is essential for designing systems that people can trust.

Looking ahead, I am particularly interested in intelligent systems, including AI and automation, and how they can be designed responsibly. My goal is to build technology that is not only innovative, but also stable, efficient, and meaningful in the long term.

『 co-curricular activities 』

Outside of my academic and professional work, I am actively involved in activities that help me develop discipline, creativity, and balance.

One of my main interests is music. I play the Kanun, a traditional Turkish string instrument, which has helped me develop focus, patience, and attention to detail. Learning and practicing music has been an important part of my personal growth and allows me to express creativity outside of technology.

I am also a professional tennis player, which has played a significant role in shaping my discipline, time management, and competitive mindset. Through tennis, I have learned how to stay consistent, handle pressure, and continuously improve through practice and dedication.

In addition, I enjoy reading fiction books. Reading allows me to escape from reality and immerse myself in different stories and perspectives.

These activities complement my academic journey by helping me stay balanced, focused, and motivated.
